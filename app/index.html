About Us

Raising Leaders, Building Futures

At Phoebestar Royalty Schools, we believe every child carries the seed of greatness. Founded in 2014 and located in the heart of Osogbo, Osun State, our institution provides a holistic education that blends academic rigor with moral upbringing. From our youngest learners in Crèche to our graduating seniors in SS3, we nurture each student to discover their potential and develop the confidence to lead.

Our serene campus at Plot M3 & M5 School Avenue features modern classrooms, well-equipped science laboratories, a digital library, sports facilities, and comfortable boarding quarters. With both day and boarding options, we serve families across Osun State and beyond.

Our Programs

Five Pathways to Excellence

From early childhood to secondary education and entrepreneurship, we provide continuous learning journeys.

Crèche

Ages 0-2 years

Our nurturing crèche provides a safe, stimulating environment where infants begin their learning journey through sensory play, music, and loving care.

  • Safe and hygienic environment
  • Qualified childcare professionals
  • Age-appropriate learning toys
  • Daily progress reports

Nursery

Ages 3-5 years

Building foundational skills through the EYFS curriculum, our nursery program develops early literacy, numeracy, and social confidence.

  • EYFS-based curriculum
  • Phonics and early reading
  • Art, music, and dance
  • Outdoor play area

Basic (Primary)

Primary 1-6

Pupils flourish in our basic school with specialized phonics classes, art & crafts, music, and a strong emphasis on core academic subjects.

  • British Primary Curriculum blend
  • ICT from Primary 1
  • Small class sizes
  • Leadership development

Secondary (JSS 1 - SS 3)

JSS 1 - SS 3

Rigorous academics combined with leadership training prepare students for WAEC, NECO, JAMB, and international examinations.

  • NERDC curriculum standard
  • Science labs and ICT center
  • Exam preparation programs
  • Career counseling

Entrepreneurship

Post-Secondary

Practical business skills, vocational training, and mentorship equip graduates for self-employment and economic independence.

  • Business plan development
  • Vocational skills training
  • Industry mentorship
  • Internship placements
